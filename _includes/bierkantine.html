De Bierkantine

After a full day at OWASP Benelux Days 2026, join us at De Bierkantine in Utrecht to relax, refuel, and connect with the security community.
Share drinks and dinner with fellow security pros and friends.

Expect craft beer, BBQ, and retro arcade games for a lively evening—and vegetarian and non-alcoholic options, too. Come for the food and fun; leave with fresh connections and great conference memories.

Spaces are limited, so reserve your spot while you can. Attendance is subject to availability.

The Bierkantine is at a 15-20 minutes walking distance from the Jaarbeurs conference center.
